# Kintra Fibers

> Kintra Fibers is a Brooklyn, New York materials-science company that makes a bio-based,
> biodegradable synthetic yarn as an alternative to petroleum polyester. Its polymer is a
> proprietary form of polybutylene succinate (PBS) made from renewable corn- and wheat-derived
> sugars and melt-spun on the same commercial equipment already used for polyester, so mills,
> spinners and extruders can adopt it without re-tooling. Kintra sells physical materials into
> the apparel supply chain, not software: as of 2026-08-23 it publishes no public API, no
> developer portal, no SDK, and no machine-readable API contract.

## Products

Kintra sells four physical products, each aimed at a different tier of the apparel supply chain:

- **Resin** — for extruders. Bio-based resin compatible with existing polyester systems.
- **Staple fiber** — for spinners. Soft, breathable fiber for blends.
- **Multifilament yarn** — for mills. 75D/72F, available as FDY, ATY and DTY.
- **Fabric** — for brands. Finished performance textiles.
